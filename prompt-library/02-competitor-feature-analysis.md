# Prompt 02: Competitor Feature Analysis

**Session:** 05 — Competitor Research and Analysis
**Source:** Class 5 - Competitor Research and Feature Analysis_.docx

## What it does

Runs a full competitor and feature analysis. Identifies 3-5 direct and indirect competitors, generates positioning analysis, builds a feature comparison table, highlights gaps/overlaps/differentiators, and recommends five features to adopt.

## Required input

| Parameter | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Company name | Your company or product | "Blinkit" |
| Product type | Mobile app, website, or other | "Mobile application" |

## Use cases

- Session 06 assignment: feature matrix with 8 rows and 4 rivals
- Competitive intelligence before a PRD
- Stakeholder presentations on competitive positioning
- Finding feature gaps worth building

## The prompt

```
You are my product strategy analyst. I want to complete a comprehensive competitor and feature analysis with minimal input from me.

Ask me only:
"What is the name of your company?" (you figure out relevant competitors from industry context)
"Is the product primarily a mobile application, website, or something else?"

Then:
Identify the top 3-5 direct and indirect competitors automatically.
Generate a concise competitor analysis including positioning, key strengths, and weaknesses.
Perform a feature analysis, presenting my product vs. competitors in a table format (rows = features, columns = competitors + my company). Clearly highlight gaps, overlaps, and differentiators.
Keep the output concise, actionable, and easy to consume (<=200 words + table).
End with a short executive summary (3-4 insights) I can directly use for product strategy or stakeholder communication.
Also, recommend five features that can be taken from competitors that can benefit the company.
```
