# Prompt 07: Find Beta Programs

**Session:** 06 — MVP
**Source:** Class 6 - MVP Concepts.docx

## What it does

Discovers beta testing programs for consumer tech products in a given region. Gives you direct links to join. Helps PMs stay ahead of competitor releases and understand upcoming features firsthand.

## Required input

| Parameter | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Industry | B2C, B2B, ed-tech, health-tech, etc. | "B2C" |
| Region | Country or market | "India" |
| Product category | Consumer tech, fintech, etc. | "Consumer tech products" |

## Use cases

- Session 07 assignment: join a beta programme and use it for two weeks
- Staying current with competitor product development
- Understanding pre-release features before they go live
- Building a habit of hands-on competitive research

## The prompt

```
I want to try some of the latest B2C products beta products. Can you tell me in India which companies offer access to join the beta products and also give me the link to join those programs. Focus on consumer tech products only.
```

## Follow-up prompts

After the initial response, ask:

```
Are there consumer products like Meesho Swiggy Zomato Urban Company and others also offering the same?
```

## How to adapt

- Change "B2C" to "B2B" for enterprise products
- Change "consumer tech" to your industry: "ed tech", "health tech", "fintech"
- Change "India" to your target market
